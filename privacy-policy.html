Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how VyomSena handles information when you use the VyomSena website and Android application (together, the “Services”). It applies to aviation personnel and to company administrators and staff using company workspaces.

Information we handle

  • Account information: name, email address, authentication identifiers, account role, and company or workspace details. Sign-in may use email and password or Google sign-in.
  • Company and aviation records: information entered by a user or their organization, which may include crew names and contact details, aircraft and flight records, licences, certificates, medical and training records, compliance details, and operational notes.
  • Files: documents and images uploaded to a workspace, such as crew or aircraft supporting documents.
  • Access and service data: module permissions, linked crew or staff records, account status, audit information, and technical data needed to authenticate users, protect accounts, and operate the Services.

How information is used

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; provide company workspaces and the modules a user is permitted to access; link authorized users to company records; store and display operational, crew, aircraft, and compliance information; send configured reminders and service messages; maintain security and audit records; and troubleshoot and improve the Services.

Organizations and other recipients

Company workspace information is available to authorized members of that organization according to their assigned access. An organization may enter information about its personnel and manage Company Home accounts. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

The Services use Google Firebase services for authentication, database, file storage, and server-side functions. Google may process information as a service provider under its applicable terms and privacy documentation. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect users, the Services, or their security.

Storage, security, and retention

Information is stored in the service infrastructure used to operate VyomSena. We use access controls and server-side checks for account and company data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained while an account or organization needs the Services and for applicable operational, security, or legal requirements. Some aviation records may be retained by an organization to meet its recordkeeping obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can ask the administrator of your company workspace to correct or remove information managed by that organization, or to suspend or delete a Company Home account they issued. For deletion requests concerning an individual VyomSena account, use the process on our Account Deletion page. Deletion may not remove records that an organization is required to keep or information that must be retained for security or legal reasons.

Children

The Services are designed for aviation professionals and organizations and are not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Services change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to future use.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email thirumalai@vyomsena.com.
