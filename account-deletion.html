Account Deletion

Effective date: October 29, 2026

VyomSena personal accounts use Google account sign-in. Personal account holders can delete their account and associated personal data from inside the Android app. Company-linked Android access is read-only and is managed by the organization.

Delete a personal account in the app

  1. Open the VyomSena Android app and go to the Account Management module.
  2. Choose the account deletion option and confirm the request while signed in to the Google account used for VyomSena.

The deletion flow removes the personal account and associated personal data from the app and its Firestore records. Some information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or an organization's recordkeeping obligations; retained information is limited to those purposes.

If you cannot access the app, email thirumalai@vyomsena.com from the Gmail address used for the account and request deletion. Do not send your password.

Company Home account (Company User ID and passcode)

  1. Company-linked Android access is view-only. Contact the administrator of the company that issued your Company User ID for access changes or account removal. Company administrators manage company users and operational data through the web app.
  2. If you cannot reach that administrator, email thirumalai@vyomsena.com with your Company User ID and company name. Do not send your passcode.

Deleting a personal VyomSena account does not delete company workspace records controlled by an organization. Company administrators handle those records, which may be retained for aviation or business recordkeeping obligations.

What happens after a request

Personal account deletion removes associated personal account data and records from the app and Firestore. Data required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or an organization's aviation recordkeeping may be retained for that purpose. Deletion does not erase company workspace records managed by the company administrator.

Need help?

For help with a deletion request, email thirumalai@vyomsena.com. This page is also available at https://vyomsena.com/account-deletion.html.
