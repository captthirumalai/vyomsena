Account Deletion

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Users can request deletion of a VyomSena account and associated personal information. The steps depend on the type of account.

Company Home account (Company User ID and passcode)

  1. Contact the administrator of the company that issued your Company User ID and ask them to delete or deactivate your account.
  2. The administrator can manage the account through Linking & Access. If you cannot reach the administrator, email thirumalai@vyomsena.com with your Company User ID and company name. Do not send your passcode.

Company Home accounts are issued and controlled by an organization. The organization may retain crew, aircraft, flight, compliance, or audit records separately from the sign-in account, including where required for aviation or business recordkeeping.

Individual VyomSena account

  1. Email thirumalai@vyomsena.com and request account and associated personal data deletion.
  2. Include the email address used to sign in and identify the VyomSena app. Do not send your password. We may ask for reasonable information to verify that you control the account.

What happens after a request

We will verify the request, remove or de-identify personal account information that we control where deletion is appropriate, and confirm when processing is complete. We may retain information where needed for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or an organization's aviation recordkeeping duties. Backups and security records may persist for a limited period before normal rotation or deletion.

Deleting an account does not automatically erase information that another user or company has entered into its workspace. Ask that organization to handle records it controls.

Need help?

For help with a deletion request, email thirumalai@vyomsena.com. This page is also available at https://vyomsena.com/account-deletion.html.
